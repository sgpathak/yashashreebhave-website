Musical Repertoire & Performance Styles

Yashashree is a versatile vocalist specializing in a rich tapestry of Indian devotional and contemporary music, including:

  • Bollywood & Filmi: Popular Hindi Bollywood hits and soulful Bollywood Ghazals.
  • Regional & Folk: Marathi film songs and traditional Marathi regional music.
  • Devotional & Spiritual: Classical and contemporary Hindi and Marathi Bhajans, interactive Bhajan jamming sessions, and specialized devotional performances for Navratri Jagran and Krishna Janmashtami.
