Short Bio for Event Collateral & Anchor Introductions

"Yashashree Bhave is an Indian playback and live concert vocalist celebrated for reaching the grand finals of Indian Idol Season 2 and securing the Final Runner-up title on Zee Sa Re Ga Ma Pa. Renowned for her vocal versatility spanning Bollywood classics, Sufi, ghazals, and high-energy contemporary numbers, she performs regularly at major music festivals, corporate galas, and cultural concerts worldwide."
