Sonu Nigam

"Legendary playback singer Sonu Nigam lauded musical artist Yashashree Bhave, hailing her as one of the most versatile vocalists emerging from Nagpur. Mesmerized by the pitch-perfect precision of her live performance—noting it sounded as flawless as a studio-recorded CD—Nigam expressed strong endorsement for her future in Bollywood playback singing."

— Sonu Nigam, Bollywood Playback Singer & Music Icon

Bappi Lahiri

"Legendary Bollywood composer and playback icon Bappi Lahiri publicly lauded singer Yashashree Bhave, spotlighting her exceptional musicality during a live performance. Praising her vocal texture, Lahiri commended Bhave for her absolute mastery over rhythm (sur), tempo (laya), and pitch-perfect delivery, calling her performance 100% complete."

— Bappi Lahiri, Music Director & Playback Pioneer

Anandji Virji Shah (Kalyanji–Anandji)

"Legendary Bollywood composer Anandji Virji Shah hailed Yashashree Bhave as a 'true playback singer.' Commending her vocal stability, emotional depth, and effortless high register, the veteran music director emphasized that her refined voice and polished tonal quality make her exceptionally suited for mainstream cinema playback."

— Anandji Virji Shah, Legendary Music Composer & Director

Farah Khan

"Acclaimed Bollywood filmmaker and choreographer Farah Khan expressed high praise for Yashashree Bhave, complimenting both her striking screen presence and melodic vocal tone. Highlighting her sweet, expressive voice as an ideal fit for Hindi cinema, Farah Khan noted that Yashashree carries the rare blend of musical skill and charismatic star appeal."

— Farah Khan, Film Director & Choreographer

Raza Murad

"Veteran Bollywood actor Raza Murad praised playback singer Yashashree Bhave, drawing comparisons between her vocal mastery and music legend Asha Bhosle. Applauding her performance of the classic song 'Aye Dil-E-Nadaan', Murad commended her vocal control, tone, and emotive delivery."

— Raza Murad, Bollywood Actor

Ashok Saraf

"Legendary theater and cinema icon Ashok Saraf praised Yashashree Bhave during the staging of his celebrated Marathi play in Nagpur. Deeply impressed by both her dynamic cultural initiatives and her soulful singing, Saraf commended Yashashree's dedication to the arts, applauding her exceptional vocal talent."

— Ashok Saraf, Legendary Film & Theater Actor

Vikram Gokhale

"Revered Indian film and theater thespian Vikram Gokhale commended Yashashree Bhave for her remarkable artistic dedication, expressive vocal range, and musical maturity. Acknowledging her deep grounding in melody (sur) and emotional storytelling, Gokhale praised her as a gifted vocalist capable of leaving a lasting impression in Indian cinema."

— Vikram Gokhale, National Award-Winning Actor & Director

Late Om Puri Sahab

"In an appreciative interaction, internationally acclaimed cinematic legend Om Puri praised Yashashree Bhave for her natural vocal clarity, warmth, and depth. Highlighting her grounded artistic sensibility, Puri noted that her voice possesses an organic, cinematic quality capable of evoking pure emotion and elevating narrative storytelling in mainstream cinema."

— Late Om Puri, Legendary Film & Theater Actor

Ali Khan

"Veteran Bollywood actor Ali Khan lauded Yashashree Bhave's vocal texture, noting that her expressive tone carries the emotive grace heard in classic Hindi cinema, reminiscent of tracks voiced for leading actresses like Sonali Bendre in Sarfarosh. Khan praised her vocal versatility and expressed enthusiasm for her mainstream projects."

— Ali Khan, Bollywood Actor
