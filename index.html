Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers regarding live bookings, vocal lessons, and professional inquiries.

How do I book Yashashree Bhave for weddings, concerts, or corporate events?

Bookings are handled directly through her official management team. Visit our Contact Us page or send a message with your event date, venue city, and show format to receive availability and fee quotes.

Are singing classes available for beginners and children?

Yes. Yashashree Bhave teaches students of all skill levels. Beginners focus on foundational swaras, pitch accuracy, and breathing, while advanced students study playback dynamics, mic technique, and emotional delivery.

What equipment is required for online singing classes?

All you need is a stable internet connection, a laptop or tablet, a decent pair of headphones, and a quiet room for riyaz. Classes take place via Zoom or Google Meet with personalized feedback.

Can Yashashree perform with her own live band?

Yes. Yashashree travels with her full professional live band setup for large-scale concerts, as well as acoustic setups or high-quality karaoke setups for intimate events.
