Structured Training Curriculum

Whether you are preparing for music board exams, reality shows, or looking to master professional playback singing, our structured curriculum adapts to your skill level:

  • Module 1: Swara Identification & Pitch Precision (Sur)
    Eliminate vocal shake and pitch inaccuracy. Focus on Kharaj ka Riyaz (lower octave training), steady note-holding on the tanpura, and microtone pitch detection.
  • Module 2: Breath Control & Diaphragmatic Support
    Techniques to sing long phrases without fatigue. Expand lung stamina, control dynamics between soft falsetto/meend and full-throated chest resonance.
  • Module 3: Harkats, Murkis, Gamaks & Exam Syllabus
    Transitioning from flat singing to soulful texture. Learn swift semi-classical inflections, sargam agility, and vocal ornamentations required for light singing classes exams and classical certifications.
  • Module 4: Bollywood Playback, Pronunciation & Studio Delivery
    Studio microphone etiquette, Urdu/Hindi diction precision (Nuktas), emotional phrasing, and recording industry prep for professional vocal auditions.
  • Module 5: Stage Performance & Reality Show Prep
    Conquering performance anxiety, stage mic handling, live monitor synchronization, and targeted competition audition song selection based on 25+ years of live concert and Indian Idol experience.
