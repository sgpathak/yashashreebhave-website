Official Artist Biography

ABOUT YASHASHREE BHAVE

Bollywood playback singer, live concert artist, and vocal mentor renowned for vocal versatility across Hindi cinema, semi-classical ballads, and regional music.

Yashashree Bhave (affectionately known as Yashi) rose to national prominence as a finalist on Sony Entertainment Television’s landmark reality series Indian Idol Season 2 and as a final runner-up on Zee TV’s Sa Re Ga Ma Pa. Over a career spanning more than 30 years and 3,500 live concerts worldwide, she has established herself as one of India's most adaptable and technically sound female vocalists.

Early Life & Classical Training

Born on August 5, 1981, in Satara, Maharashtra, Yashashree was raised in a culturally rich family environment. Her mother was an accomplished classical singer, and her father, Vijay Bhave, was an ardent patron of the arts who managed theatre productions in Mumbai.

Recognizing her innate pitch and rhythm at the early age of four, her formal vocal training began under classical gurus including Rajani Aurangabadkar, Pramilatai Sathe, and Madhavi Mhaisalkar. She delivered her debut public stage performance on June 7, 1992, at the Dr. Vasantrao Deshpande Hall in Nagpur, commencing an artistic trajectory that grew from regional auditoriums to international arena stages.

National Television Breakthrough

  • Sa Re Ga Ma Pa (Zee Marathi, 2001)
    Clinched the Final Runner-Up position, earning unanimous acclaim from the celebrity jury and viewers across Maharashtra for her swara clarity, raag precision, and mastery over Natyasangeet.
  • Indian Idol Season 2 (Sony Entertainment Television, 2005–2006)
    Emerged as a national Top Finalist among over 50,000 contestants. Her audition rendition of Lata Mangeshkar’s classic "Ruke Ruke Se Kadam" earned an immediate standing ovation from celebrity judge Sonu Nigam. In subsequent gala rounds, her performances of "Piya Bawri" and "Kehna Hi Kya" led Sonu Nigam to remark that her delivery matched K.S. Chithra's original vocal perfection.

Playback Discography & Releases

Yashashree has recorded soundtracks, regional playback, and commercial singles under leading music banners:

  • Bollywood & Marathi Cinema Playback
    Playback credits for feature films including Ek Hakikat Ganga ("Chhoti Si Umar"), Bhala Manus, Roopachi Roopwanti, and Ye Sawakara.
  • Zee Music Commercial Singles
    The semi-classical solo album "Mai Toh Ho Gai Re Sajna Teri" and the high-energy modern pop single "Kudi Mai Kaamal Di".
  • Sony BMG Multi-Platinum Album
    Lead solo vocals on "Ae Dil-E-Nadan" and ensemble lead on the Indian Idol Season 2 signature title anthem "Woh Pehli Baar".
  • Devotional Albums
    Sacred albums including Chalo Jaye Maiyya Ke Dwar, Pyara Saja Darbar, and revered classical stotras.

3,500+ Live Concerts

Leading her musical setup, Y.B Musical Events and Orchestra, Yashashree delivers versatile concert programs ranging from Kishore-Lata tributes and retro Bollywood melodies to energetic Punjabi tracks, Marathi Bhavgeet, and Bhajan Jamming across India, the Middle East (Kuwait, UAE), and global destinations.

Vocal Mentorship & Jury

Beyond performance, Yashashree serves as an expert adjudicator for state and national singing competitions. She trains aspiring singers through structured online classes worldwide and in-studio batches in Nagpur covering classical riyaz, breath mechanics, sur alignment, and studio mic dynamics.
